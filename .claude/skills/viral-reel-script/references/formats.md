# Format library — pick ONE per reel, matched to the goal

Each format is a plug-and-play skeleton. Fill the brackets with the topic. Pick by GOAL.

## PSA / "Problem → Shift → Payoff" — goal: authority, saves
- HOOK: "If you keep struggling with [X], this might be why."
- PROBLEM: name the common mistake the viewer recognizes.
- SHIFT: the better way to think about it.
- PAYOFF: the result or lesson.
- CTA: "Save this for when you need it."
- Best for: coaches, educators, service businesses, personal brands.

## Before / After / Lesson — goal: transformation, trust
- BEFORE: "This used to feel hard because [reason]."
- CHANGE: "Then I started doing [thing]."
- AFTER: "Now the result is [outcome]."
- LESSON: "The key was [insight]."
- CTA: "Try this next time you [situation]."
- Best for: fitness, beauty, business, productivity, creator journeys, case studies.

## Listicle — goal: retention, saves
- HOOK: "3 simple ways to [outcome]."
- TIP 1: easiest fix first. TIP 2: the meatier one. TIP 3: the surprising/best one (tease it in the hook so they stay for it).
- CTA: save / share.
- Best for: high-utility, quick wins, "X things you didn't know".

## POV / relatable — goal: shares, relatability
- Open with on-screen "POV: [situation the audience lives]."
- Play out the scene; land on the emotional truth or twist.
- Best for: humor, niche in-jokes, "us vs them" relatability. Drives shares.

## Myth vs Fact — goal: authority, comments
- HOOK: "Stop believing [common myth] about [topic]."
- MYTH: state it plainly. FACT: correct it with proof.
- CTA: "Comment if you believed this too."
- Best for: debunking, expertise, sparking mild debate in comments.

## Storytime / open-loop narrative — goal: watch-through, shares
- Open at the most suspenseful or unexpected moment (lead with the weird detail).
- Tell the story; hold the resolution till the end; pay it off with a satisfying twist.
- Best for: behind-the-scenes, real experiences, "you won't believe what happened".

## Tutorial / How-to — goal: saves, authority
- HOOK: "Here's exactly how to [result] in [timeframe]."
- STEP 1 → STEP 2 → STEP 3 (each a micro-loop).
- PAYOFF: show the finished result.
- CTA: "Save this / comment '[WORD]' for the template."
- Best for: skills, recipes, setups, processes.

## UGC / authentic product — goal: soft conversion, organic feel
- Close-up detail: texture/reaction/usage.
- Experience line: "I didn't expect it to work this fast."
- Proof: a stat, testimonial line, or visible result.
- Soft close: "DM '[WORD]' to pick the right option."
- Best for: products/services without a hard-sell feel. Most trusted format in 2026.

## Visual-breakdown / faceless — goal: retention on B-roll
- HOOK (voiceover): "This is the simple reason [X] happens."
- SCENE 1: show the problem. SCENE 2: show the process/contrast. SCENE 3: show the result.
- VOICEOVER PAYOFF: "The real lesson is [insight]."
- CTA: "Follow for more simple breakdowns."
- Best for: aesthetic/faceless/AI-visual/screen-recording reels.

---
### Picking the format fast
- Want **saves** → PSA, Listicle, Tutorial.
- Want **shares** → POV, Storytime, Before/After.
- Want **comments** → Myth vs Fact, opinion-led PSA.
- Want **soft sales** → UGC, Tutorial-with-keyword-DM.
- Faceless / no on-camera → Visual-breakdown, faceless Tutorial.
