# Techniques: hooks, psychology, pacing, CTA, algorithm

## 1. The hook (0–2 seconds) — where 50% of drop-off lives

Rules:
- 7–12 words, ~1–2 seconds spoken. Short hooks reduce skips.
- Benefit first. Lead with the outcome, name who it's for, hint at the method/proof.
- Mirror the hook as on-screen text in the first frame.
- No preamble. "Hey guys / سلام دوستان / so today I want to talk about…" kills retention.
- The hook must honestly preview the content (no bait-and-switch).

### Hook formulas (steal and fill the brackets)
- **POV:** "POV: you just found the [category] hack that saves you [time/money]."
- **Specific audience call-out:** "If you're a [very specific person], this is the [thing] you didn't know you needed." (Specificity multiplies hook rate — "X under $50k/mo" beats "entrepreneur".)
- **Contrarian / negative:** "Stop using [popular method]. Here's what actually works."
- **Mistake:** "The biggest mistake even [experts] make with [topic]."
- **Confession:** "I was today years old when I found out [surprising fact]…"
- **Listicle promise:** "3 [things] that [outcome]." / "Here are 3 ways to [goal]."
- **If/then:** "If you want [result], do this." / "Stop doing [mistake], do this instead."
- **Curiosity gap / open loop:** "This one [thing] changed everything — and it's not what you think."
- **Numbered tease:** "The third one will surprise you." / "Wait till the 10-second mark."
- **Visual/text mismatch:** on-screen text says one thing while the video shows another, creating a gap the viewer must resolve. ("What I say to clients vs. what I'm thinking.")
- **Question:** pose a sharp question, promise the answer later.
- **Story open:** start a story at its most suspenseful moment, resolve at the end.

### Power words that trigger response
secret, mistake, warning, nobody tells you, actually, the truth about, this is why, stop, before you, the real reason.

## 2. Psychology (the levers under the hooks)

- **Information-gap theory (Loewenstein):** a gap between what we know and want to know is uncomfortable; the brain seeks closure. Open it in the hook.
- **Zeigarnik effect:** the brain remembers and fixates on *unfinished* tasks/stories. Unresolved loops = retention.
- **Curiosity gap:** tease that something valuable/surprising is coming; reveal slowly; pay it off so it feels worth the wait.
- **Pattern interrupt:** an unexpected visual, sound, or claim resets attention and stops the scroll.
- **Self-identification / personal relevance:** "If you have [trait]…" — viewers stay when they feel seen.
- **Emotion drives shares:** awe, surprise, healthy fear ("avoid this mistake"), and joy get sent to friends. Lead with feeling, not dry facts.
- **Social currency:** people share content that says something about *them*. Make the viewer look smart/good for sharing.
- **Tension & release:** build suspense, then deliver the insight. Oscillate emotional states.
- **Avoid reactance:** overtly manipulative hooks ("you WON'T BELIEVE!!!") trigger resistance and underperform. Authentic curiosity beats clickbait.

## 3. Structure & pacing

- **Micro-loops:** break the body into small segments, each ending on a tiny curiosity gap ("before I show the result, here's why most people fail at this step…").
- **Staircasing / escalating stakes:** each beat should feel like something bigger is coming; raise the stakes as the video progresses.
- **Skip exposition:** characterization through action, backstory revealed *after* interest is secured. Jump straight into the core idea.
- **Re-hook the middle:** around the natural drop-off point, spark curiosity again so viewers don't leave.
- **Strong outro:** summarize the payoff, remind them of the problem you solved. The end is where likes/comments/shares/saves happen.

## 4. CTA menu (pick ONE, keep it on-platform)

Saves and shares are the strongest distribution signals. Prefer CTAs that keep the
viewer *in* the app rather than "link in bio".

- **Save:** "Save this before you [need it]." (best for educational reels)
- **Share:** "Send this to someone who [situation]."
- **Comment (specific):** instead of "follow for more", ask "Which one would you pick?"
  or "Has anyone else tried this?" — specificity drives comments.
- **Keyword DM:** "Comment '[WORD]' and I'll send you the [resource]."
- **Soft sell:** "DM '[WORD]' if you want help choosing." (value first, pitch last)
- Note: a trend-only / pure-entertainment reel may need no CTA — let the content lead.

## 5. The 2026 algorithm — what actually gets pushed

- **Distribution is decided by the first ~3 seconds** (Reels ads guidance even says 0.5s). Weak/late hook = suppressed reach.
- **Top signals:** shares > watch-through rate > saves > replays > comments. A reel with high shares but low likes is still a win.
- **Originality is a primary ranking factor** (post Sept-2025 changes); recycled trends lost 40–60% of reach.
- **Raw/authentic beats over-polished.** Content that looks like an ad before it's useful gets scrolled.
- **Never cross-post with a competitor watermark** (e.g. TikTok logo) — 40–60% reach penalty.
- **Specs:** 9:16, 1080×1920, keep hook/face/text in the centered "safe zone", always add captions, design a clean cover frame for the profile grid.
- **Completion rate is king:** ~72% completion at 15s vs ~46% at 60s. Shorter is safer.
- **Hook rate stabilizes in 24–48h:** if a reel opens under ~20% hook rate, replace the hook and re-test — body fixes won't save it.

## 6. Reverse-engineering virals (research move)

Don't brainstorm from zero. Find a reel already performing (in or out of the niche),
identify the exact hook structure it used, and map that same structural dynamic onto
the user's topic. Same mechanics, new application.
